About City General Hospital

Serving the community with compassionate, high-quality healthcare for over 30 years.

Our Story

Founded in 1990, City General Hospital began as a small community clinic with a single mission: to provide accessible, compassionate healthcare to every person, regardless of their background or financial situation.

Over three decades, we have grown into a full-service hospital with over 50 specialists, state-of-the-art surgical suites, and a 24/7 emergency department — but our core values remain unchanged.

Today, we serve over 10,000 patients annually and continue to expand our services to meet the evolving health needs of our community.
